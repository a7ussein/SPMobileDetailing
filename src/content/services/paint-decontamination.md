Even after a thorough wash, your vehicle's paint can feel rough due to embedded iron particles, tree sap, and industrial fallout. Our **Paint Decontamination** service removes these impurities using specialized chemical iron removers and a physical clay bar treatment.

### Why You Need It:

- Prepares the surface for waxes, sealants, or ceramic coatings
- Prevents long-term clear coat damage
- Restores a perfectly smooth surface

_Recommended as an add-on to our Exterior Wash before applying any protection._
