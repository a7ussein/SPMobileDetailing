Our **Exterior Detail** goes well beyond a basic car wash. We start with a pre-rinse and foam bath to safely lift dirt, followed by a careful hand wash using paint-conscious media that won't leave swirl marks.

Wheels and tires are scrubbed clean and dressed, bug splatter and tar spots are removed, and the finish is sealed with a spray wax for lasting protection and gloss. All exterior glass is cleaned for crystal-clear visibility.

The result is a vehicle that looks like it just rolled off the lot — without the risk of a tunnel wash.
