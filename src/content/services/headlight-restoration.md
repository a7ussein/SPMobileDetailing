Cloudy or yellowed headlights not only age the look of your vehicle but also significantly reduce nighttime visibility, making driving dangerous. Our **Headlight Restoration** service permanently removes the oxidized layer and restores factory clarity.

### The Process:

- Taping and protecting the surrounding paint
- Multi-stage wet sanding to remove heavy oxidation
- Machine compounding and polishing for crystal clarity
- Application of a dedicated UV ceramic coating to prevent future fading

_Enjoy safe, bright driving at night._
