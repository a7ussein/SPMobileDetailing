## What this project is

SP Mobile Detailing is a mobile car-detailing business in Maine, owned and operated by
Ahmad Alsaleh (20). The service comes to the customer (driveway / workplace / lot) and
covers exterior wash, interior detail, paint decontamination, ceramic-style sealants,
and headlight restoration. Audience: Maine vehicle owners who value convenience and a
premium finish — daily drivers, enthusiasts, and small fleets.

Stack: Astro 5 + Tailwind v4 + React islands (studio template).

## Brand

- **Name:** SP Mobile Detailing
- **Owner:** Ahmad Alsaleh

## Client-specific rules

- Lead generation is the #1 goal: visible phone CTA + booking form on every page.
- Maine-local SEO: city pages for Portland, Bangor, Lewiston, Augusta, South Portland.
- Showcase work via before/after photo blocks once real photos exist — no stock cars.
- Mobile-first layout — most traffic will be on phones.

## Do NOT

- No generic stock photos of luxury cars the business hasn't actually detailed.
- No GA, chatbots, or trust-badge clutter unless Ahmad asks.
- No placeholder copy at ship time.
